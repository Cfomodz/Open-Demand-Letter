Non-Exemption of For-Profit Solicitors:
Please be advised that your status as a for-profit entity, even when soliciting on behalf of a non-profit organization, does not exempt you from the Telemarketing Sales Rule (TSR) or the TCPA. Under 16 C.F.R. § 310.4(b)(1)(iii)(B), it is an abusive telemarketing act for a telemarketer to initiate an outbound call to a person on the National Do Not Call Registry. The FTC's guidance, Complying with the Telemarketing Sales Rule, states that while non-profits are exempt, "for-profit companies that solicit charitable donations on behalf of non-profit organizations" must comply with the Do Not Call provisions when they are engaging in "telemarketing" as defined by the Rule.
